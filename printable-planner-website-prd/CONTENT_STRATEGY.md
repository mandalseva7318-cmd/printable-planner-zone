## Pinterest Strategy

### Pin Creation Tips
1. **Vertical Images** (2:3 ratio - 1000x1500px recommended)
2. **Text Overlay** on preview images
3. **Consistent Branding** - use your color palette
4. **Multiple Pin Designs** for same planner (A/B testing)

### Pin Title Formula
- "Free [Type] Planner Printable PDF"
- "[Benefit] + [Type] + Printable"
- Examples:
  - "Free Minimal Daily Planner Printable PDF"
  - "Stay Organized Weekly Planner Printable"
  - "Budget Tracker Printable for Saving Money"

### Pin Description Template
```
[Planner Name] - Free Printable PDF

Perfect for [target audience], this [adjective] planner helps you [benefit].

✨ Features:
• [Feature 1]
• [Feature 2]
• [Feature 3]

📥 Download for free and print at home!

#printable #planner #[category] #organization #productivity
```

### Pinterest Board Structure
1. **Daily Planners** (20+ pins)
2. **Weekly Planners** (20+ pins)
3. **Monthly Planners** (20+ pins)
4. **Budget & Finance** (20+ pins)
5. **Study & Student** (20+ pins)
6. **Meal Planning** (20+ pins)
7. **Health & Fitness** (20+ pins)
8. **Habit Trackers** (20+ pins)
9. **Bullet Journal Inspiration** (curated content)
10. **Productivity Tips** (curated content)

### Pinning Schedule
- **Tailwind** or **Pinterest Scheduler**
- Pin **5-10 times per day**
- Space pins **2-3 hours apart**
- Repin old content weekly
